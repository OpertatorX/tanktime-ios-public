Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

TankTime is designed as a local-first utility. Tank profiles, appliance settings and calculation history that you create are stored on your device and are not sent to an OperatorX account or backend.

Advertising

TankTime is supported by Google Mobile Ads. The app requests non-personalized ad treatment. Before eligible ad requests, TankTime may ask for Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission so Google Mobile Ads can access a device advertising identifier for advertising and ad measurement when you allow it. You can decline and continue using TankTime.

Google and its advertising partners may still process information needed to deliver, secure and measure ads, including IP-derived approximate location, device or app identifiers, advertising interactions, diagnostics and performance information. Google User Messaging Platform is used to present consent or privacy choices where required.

Tracking

Google Mobile Ads declares that a device identifier may be linked to you and used for tracking for third-party advertising, developer advertising and analytics. TankTime therefore requests ATT before allowing access to tracking identifiers. Personalized ad treatment remains disabled in TankTime, and if you deny ATT the Google Mobile Ads SDK does not receive the IDFA.

OperatorX data collection

OperatorX does not require an account for TankTime and does not operate a server that receives your saved tank profiles, trip plans or calculation history.

Your choices

You can decline the ATT request, change tracking permission later in iOS Settings, and manage available advertising privacy choices from TankTime when required for your region. You can remove locally stored TankTime data by deleting app entries or uninstalling the app.

Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect product, legal or advertising-platform changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Support

For privacy questions, email contact.operatorx@proton.me or use the TankTime support page.
