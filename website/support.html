TankTime Support

TankTime estimates fuel remaining from a scale reading and estimates runtime from the appliance load you enter.

Runtime calculator

If a result looks wrong, confirm that the scale weight includes the complete tank, that the tare weight comes from its marking, and that the appliance consumption rating uses the expected unit.

Trip planner

Add the appliances you expect to use, enter realistic hours per day, choose a reserve, and use the current tank weight. TankTime estimates daily burn, total fuel required and extra full tanks.

Privacy choices

If privacy options are required in your region, open TankTime → Settings → Manage ad privacy choices.

Need more help?
